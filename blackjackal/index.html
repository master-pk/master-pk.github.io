Privacy Policy — Blackjackal

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Blackjackal is a single-player card game. This policy explains what data the app

handles. The short version: **Blackjackal collects nothing, sends nothing, and

has no account.**

Information we collect

None.

There is no sign-in, no account, and no registration. We do not collect your

name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or

any device identifier. We do not use analytics. We do not use advertising.

Information stored on your device

Your progress is stored only on your device and is never transmitted

anywhere:

  • Your current run — the deck, your charms, the blind you are on
  • Progression — furthest ante reached, difficulty rung unlocked, charms

discovered, daily streak

  • Cosmetics you have earned, and which card back is equipped
  • Settings — sound, music, haptics, assist mode
  • Whether you have completed the tutorial

This lives in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling the app, clearing its

data, or using Wipe Progress in Settings removes it permanently. There is no

cloud save and no cross-device sync, so progress does not survive a reinstall.

Network activity

Blackjackal makes no network requests. The game is fully playable offline

and does not contact any server, including ours. There is nothing to intercept

and nothing to leak.

Children

Blackjackal contains simulated card play with no real-money gambling, no wagers,

and no cash prizes. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data

from children.

Purchases

Blackjackal is offered with a free portion and a single one-time purchase that

unlocks the full game. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the

Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The store tells

the app only whether the unlock has been bought.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that touches the network — online lobbies, for

example — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the

"last updated" date above will change. We will not quietly begin collecting

data under an old policy.
